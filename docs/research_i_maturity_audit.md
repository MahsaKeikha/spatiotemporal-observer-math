# Research I maturity audit

## Scope

This audit records what the repository currently establishes and what remains a
scientific limitation. It is not a venue or submission plan.

## Established computational chain

The extension now connects candidate moving-boundary scores to exact world-tube
optimization, runner-up comparison, covariance-relative perturbation bounds,
factorwise local and transport uncertainty, strict robust separation, finite
sample Wishart calibration, certification-aware measurement, observational
equivalence, assumption auditing, targeted repair, and independent held-out
certification provenance.

The certificate uses strict separation. Equality at zero does not certify.

Adaptive design data and certification data are explicitly separated.
Independence is a provenance assertion supplied by the experimenter, not
something the software can prove from a Boolean flag. The finite-sample
certificate is driven by effective innovation degrees of freedom r=N-q.

Repairs accumulate stale-state flags. Appending observations does not by itself
make a stale structural certificate valid. Structural validity is restored only
after an explicit rebuild. Log-likelihood-ratio increments are signed finite
random quantities.

## Scientific boundary

The observer scores are operational dynamical quantities. They can support
consciousness-related measurement hypotheses, but they do not by themselves
establish that a subsystem is conscious.

The active-measurement extension does not claim invention of KL divergence,
sequential likelihood testing, controlled sensing, mutual information,
conditional mutual information, canonical correlation, Jaccard continuity,
dynamic programming, or Wishart concentration. The contribution under study is
their specific coupling to moving subsystem-boundary inference, robust
world-tube certification, equivalence-aware abstention, and targeted
reacquisition.

## Closest neighboring problem families

The work should be compared explicitly with dynamic community detection,
temporal-network tracking, controlled sensing for sequential multihypothesis
testing, active experimental design, covariance concentration, and dynamic
support/subspace tracking. Similarity in one component does not establish
equivalence of the complete inference-and-certification chain.

## Remaining hard checks

1. Execute the complete test suite in a clean environment and archive the run.
2. Execute the analytical moving-module critical-radius benchmark and retain
   the deterministic JSON result rather than hand-entering values.
3. Verify the exact leader and runner-up actions against the canonical
   world-tube optimizer.
4. Run hard negative controls: observational equivalence, near-equivalence,
   candidate misspecification, dropout, heterogeneous noise, and deliberately
   broken temporal/covariance assumptions.
5. Complete a claim-by-claim literature comparison before making priority or
   novelty claims.
6. Treat large sufficient sample counts as theorem diagnostics, not empirical
   requirements, and report the factorwise conservatism decomposition.

## Readiness rule

The repository is scientifically mature for a full paper draft when the clean
test run, deterministic benchmark record, hard negative controls, and
claim-level literature audit are all present and internally consistent. Until
then, the mathematical development is strong but the evidence package remains
in validation.
